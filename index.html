Act IScene 01

The origin story

Every design has a backstory. Here's mine — no radioactive spiders, just a lot of curiosity, coffee and cinema.

Every hero has an origin story. Mine started with a blank canvas, a movie obsession, and a radioactive question: what if design could feel like a scene?

I'm Aswin — a versatile graphic designer with 1+ year of experience across graphic design and UI/UX. I'm proficient in Adobe Creative Suite and Figma, and I speak fluent brand, poster and interface.

From branding, logos and print to user research, wireframes and prototypes, I work across the entire storyboard — obsessing over the details until the final frame feels right. When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm probably deep in a movie marathon or hunting for my next series.
